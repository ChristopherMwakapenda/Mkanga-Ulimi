GOAT PRODUCTION

Building Superior Goat Genetics

Mkanga Ulimi specialises in Boer, Savanna and quality local goat breeds. Our breeding programme focuses on strong genetics, rapid growth, fertility and disease resistance.

Through proper feeding, health management and responsible breeding, we are steadily expanding our herd to become one of Malawi's trusted suppliers of quality breeding stock.

OUR COMMITMENT

Healthy Animals. Quality Products.

Our livestock production is built on the belief that healthy animals produce better products.

Through quality genetics, good nutrition, proper hygiene and responsible animal husbandry, we are working to build a livestock enterprise that benefits consumers, farmers and the wider agricultural sector.

THE FUTURE

Growing Our Livestock Enterprise

We continue to expand our livestock production and improve our genetics as we work towards becoming a leading breeder and supplier of quality livestock in Malawi.
