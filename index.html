ABOUT MKANGA-ULIMI

Building sustainable agriculture to contribute to Malawi's growth

Mkanga-Ulimi Farm is a contemporary Malawian-owned agricultural enterprise dedicated to growing Malawi's future through sustainable agriculture. Located at the foot of Mkanga Hill in Suzi Village, Traditional Authority Kambwiri in Salima District, we produce high-quality livestock, poultry, fruits, vegetables and other farm products using sustainable and environmentally responsible farming practices.

We combine modern agricultural techniques with local knowledge to provide healthy food, create employment opportunities and strengthen rural livelihoods. Beyond production, Mkanga-Ulimi Farm offers agricultural advisory services, supports community development and inspires young people to see agriculture as a profitable, innovative and rewarding career that contributes to Malawi's food security and economic growth.
