OUR ORCHARD

Growing Fruit. Growing Opportunity.

Building a productive commercial orchard for the future.

OUR ORCHARD ENTERPRISE

Growing Fruit for the Future

Orchard production is an important part of Mkanga Ulimi's diversified agricultural enterprise.

Our orchard activities include mango production and soursop, with mangoes forming the main focus of our commercial fruit production.

We are developing our orchard with a long-term vision of producing high-quality fruit for local and commercial markets while creating opportunities for future value addition.

Our approach combines selected fruit varieties, reliable irrigation, organic manure and responsible orchard management.

OUR MAIN ORCHARD ENTERPRISE

Mangoes

Building a Commercial Mango Orchard

We launched our mango venture during the 2021–2022 rainy season, when 300 seedlings were planted.

Since then, every year we have continued to add more trees while maintaining and caring for those already established.

Tommy Atkins and Kent are our two main mango varieties at the farm. The choice of these varieties was informed by our research, which shows that they are highly sought after.

We currently have a population of XXX mango trees , of which XXX have reached the fruiting stage . The remaining trees are expected to begin bearing fruit from the 2027/2028 season.

At full production capacity, we expect to harvest a minimum of XXX tonnes of mangoes.

Malawi Mangoes is our primary buyer. As production grows, we will continue exploring other potential buyers and market opportunities.

Value addition to our mango produce is our ultimate goal. Once the orchard reaches full production capacity, we will begin exploring opportunities to process and add value to our mangoes.

WATER MANAGEMENT

Drip Irrigation

All our mangoes, except the newly planted trees, are under drip irrigation.

This provides an efficient and reliable supply of water to the orchard and supports the healthy growth and development of our mango trees.

SOIL FERTILITY

Organic Manure

We use organic manure for fertilisation of our mango trees.

This forms part of our commitment to producing quality food while minimising the use of artificial fertilisers and other chemical inputs.

OTHER FRUIT PRODUCTION

Soursop

Soursop is another fruit tree grown at Mkanga Ulimi as part of our efforts to diversify our orchard enterprise.

The crop forms part of our broader vision to develop a diversified fruit production system that contributes to healthy food production and creates additional opportunities for commercial agriculture.

As our orchard continues to develop, we will continue exploring opportunities to expand fruit production and develop value-added products.
