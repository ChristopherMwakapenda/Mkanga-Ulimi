Growing agriculture. Growing opportunity. Growing together.

WHO WE ARE

Rooted in Malawi. Growing for the Future.

At the foot of Mkanga Hill in Suzi Village, Traditional Authority Kambwiri in Salima District, is Mkanga Ulimi — a contemporary Malawian-owned semi-organic mixed farm.

Located approximately 20 kilometres off the Salima-Blantyre M5 Road, Mkanga Ulimi is a diversified agricultural enterprise producing livestock, poultry, fruits, vegetables and natural honey.

We also provide agricultural training and advisory services to individuals, organisations, groups and communities, helping people improve agricultural productivity, marketing and livelihoods.

Mkanga Ulimi is registered with the Malawi Registrar of Companies as a sole proprietorship.

OUR JOURNEY

How It All Started

The idea of Mkanga Ulimi was conceived in 2018 by Daud Kayisi. Land acquisition began in 2019 and the farm has continued to acquire land parcels surrounding the original property.

Farm operations began in 2020. Since then, Mkanga Ulimi has continued to grow from an initial agricultural vision into a diversified mixed farming enterprise.

In 2021, the farm began developing its mango orchard and introduced pigs and chickens. Goats were also introduced, with the first herd arriving at the farm in October 2021.

By April 2022, the farm had acquired a parent stock of 100 goats, which have since multiplied into hundreds.

In May 2022, two boreholes were drilled to support livestock, poultry and irrigation. An additional borehole was added in 2025.

In 2026, beekeeping commenced after years of allowing the natural forest to regenerate.

OUR APPROACH

Farming with Purpose

Mkanga Ulimi believes that agriculture is more than food production. Farming can create wealth, provide employment, strengthen communities and create opportunities for innovation.

Our approach combines traditional agricultural knowledge with modern farming practices, quality genetics, good nutrition, environmental stewardship and data-driven decision-making.

Most of our goats, except Boer goats, have been purchased from surrounding communities. This provides local farmers with a ready and reliable market for their animals.

OUR VISION

Inspiring a New Generation of Farmers

To build a modern, sustainable and commercially successful agricultural enterprise that creates wealth, employment and opportunity through farming while inspiring a new generation to see agriculture as a pathway to prosperity.

OUR MISSION

Quality Farming with Purpose

At Mkanga Ulimi, we endeavour to become a modern mixed farm that produces quality livestock, poultry and horticultural products all-year round.

To achieve this, we strive to produce quality food with minimal use of chemical inputs such as artificial fertilisers, herbicides and pesticides.

We do not focus only on increasing numbers and tonnage, but also on producing healthy food and improving the quality of our genetics.

TEAM LEADER & FOUNDER

Daud Kayisi

Daud Kayisi is a trained journalist with a career spanning more than 15 years working with international organisations as a media and communications expert.

Born and raised in a smallholder subsistence farming family, Daud has always been passionate about agriculture and believes that everyone can participate in farming at different levels.

Driven by the belief that farming has the power to create wealth, improve livelihoods and strengthen communities, Daud established Mkanga Ulimi in 2019 to demonstrate that agriculture is more than food production.

Under his leadership, Mkanga Ulimi continues to embrace sustainable farming practices, modern technologies and data-driven decision-making to improve productivity and create lasting impact.

His vision remains clear: to inspire a new generation of farmers, promote just commercial agriculture and empower surrounding communities with small farming techniques to improve their economic status.

“If Malawi is an agrobased economy, why is the majority of the youth on social media and not farm?”

— Daud Kayisi, Founder of Mkanga Ulimi
